## Categories

Open **Categories**. Press **Add Category** and enter a **Label**, an optional **Parent Category** (leave it as None for a top-level one), a **Page Title**, a **Subtitle**, a **Description** and an **Image**.

Only one person edits categories at a time. If someone else is editing, you are shown who and when they started. When you are finished, press **Save**. **Restore** undoes all your changes while you are editing. After 30 minutes of inactivity the lock releases, your changes stay, but you can no longer restore the original.

## Brands

Open **Brands** and add one with a **Brand Name**.

- Tick **Create a page for this brand** to give it its own page, listing all of its products.
- Add an **External Info URL** if the brand has a page you want to link to.
- Upload a **Logo**: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP or SVG, up to 10MB.

The brand page's title and description are edited in **Pages**.
