Open **Osmium Shop Settings**.

## Shop settings

- **Enable Product Search** shows a search icon in the header.
- **Currency Code** and **Currency Symbol**, **Tax Rate (%)**, **Tax Name** and **Country** (a two-letter code such as GB).
- **Delivery Message** is shown to customers.
- **Basket Abandoned Threshold (hours)** marks baskets with no activity for that long as abandoned.
- **Order Reference Prefix** starts every order reference, so NC- gives NC-1001, NC-1002 and so on. It applies to new orders only.

## Customer notifications

Choose how customers are told about their order. Texts need an SMS service installed from the Osmium Store and set up. They are charged by your provider, and orders with no phone number are skipped.

**Order Confirmation Note** is an optional extra line added to the confirmation email.

## What pages show

Under **Category Pages**, choose whether to show product and subcategory counts, summary text and a call to action, and how many **Products per page** (100 shows them all).

Under **Product Pages**, choose whether to show the summary, the price excluding tax and the delivery message.

Press **Save Settings**.
