A product's status says whether customers can buy it and what they are told. **Statuses** lets you create your own, such as In stock, Pre-order or Discontinued.

## Add a status

Press **Create Status** and fill in:

- **Short Description:** a brief label, up to 20 characters.
- **Long Description:** the full wording shown to customers, up to 50 characters.
- **Schema Reference:** the matching search engine availability value.
- **Product Available:** can customers order a product with this status?
- **Restocking:** is the item being restocked?
- **Show in Lists:** should products with this status appear in product lists?
- **Enabled:** is this status available to use?

Press **Create Status**.

If you use Stock Control, choose one status that allows buying and one that does not, so products move between them as stock changes.
