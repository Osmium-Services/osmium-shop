Open **Orders**. The tiles at the top show orders **Awaiting dispatch** (paid, not yet sent), **Paid, last 30 days**, **Pending** (started, never paid) and **Refunded**. You can filter the list by status.

## Dispatch an order

1. Open the order. It lists the items, quantities, VAT, any discount, delivery and **Total paid**.
2. Under **Fulfilment**, enter the **Carrier** and the **Tracking reference**.
3. Press **Mark dispatched**. The customer is emailed their tracking details.

If the email fails to send, you are told, and the order is still marked dispatched.

## Print paperwork

Use **Print invoice** or **Print despatch note** on the order.

## Test payments

An order paid through a payment provider's test mode shows **Test payment**. No real money was taken, and it cannot be marked dispatched.
