## Change the price

Open the product and go to the **Sales** tab. Enter the **Price** excluding or including VAT, and the other is worked out. You can also set the **RRP**, and a **Custom Offer Text** such as "Buy 2 Save 10%".

**History** next to Pricing lists every price change with the old price, the new price, where it came from and who made it.

## Change availability

On the same tab, choose the **Product Status**. The status decides whether customers can order it. If it is out of stock, set an **Expected Restock Date**, which is shown to customers on the product page.

To use your own wording for a status, see the article on product statuses.

## Many products at once

The **Products** list can be filtered by **Category**, **Brand** and **Status**. Use **Duplicate** to copy a product as a starting point for a similar one. The copy gets its own page and a copy of the images, and starts as a **Draft**, so customers can't see it until you set **Visibility** to **Published** on its Sales tab. A Draft is hidden from lists, search and the sitemap, and its page shows a 404 to everyone except a signed-in admin.
