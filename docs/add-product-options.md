Options let a customer choose between versions of one product, such as a size or a controller type. Add them on the product's **Options** tab.

## Add an option

1. Press **Add Option Type** and enter a **Label** such as Size or Colour.
2. Choose a **Default Option**, the choice selected when the page opens.
3. Press **Add Option Value** for each choice and fill in:
   - **Description:** what the customer sees.
   - **Premium +/- (£):** how much it adds to, or takes off, the price.
   - **RRP (£)** and **Weight +/- (kg)**.
   - **Reference / SKU**.
   - **Linked Product (optional):** ties the choice to a real product, so its stock is checked.
   - **Linked Image (optional):** the main picture changes to this one when it is chosen.

## Useful extras

- **Product Behind Default Option (optional)** is the product the customer gets if they keep the default, so its stock can be checked.
- **Linked Image for Default Option (optional)** swaps the main image when the default is selected.
- **Only show when... (optional)** hides a whole option until the shopper picks one of the values you tick.
- **Copy from...** copies option types from another product. Choose the **Source Product** and **Copy Options**.

Prices and weights for options can be changed in bulk with a spreadsheet. See the article on updating prices from a spreadsheet.

## Related products

Below the options, **See Also** links a related product such as a kit, and **Alternative products** shows a carousel of other products on the product page in the order you set.
