## Create the product

Open **Products** and add a new one. A short guide takes you through three steps.

1. **Identity:** choose the **Brand** and enter the **Model / Product Name**. The page address is built from these, shown as `/brand-model/`.
2. **Category:** choose the **Primary Category**. This decides where the product appears on the site. You can also pick a **Parent Page** to change the breadcrumb trail.
3. **Pricing:** enter the **Price**, either excluding or including VAT (the other is worked out), and choose the **Stock Status**.

Then create the product. If you change your mind straight away, **Undo Creation** removes the product, its page and its images.

## Finish it off

The product page has tabs:

- **Details:** brand, model name, **Barcode**, **Reference / SKU**, categories.
- **Sales:** prices, **RRP**, **Custom Offer Text** such as "Buy 2 Save 10%", **Product Status** and **Packed Weight (kg)** for shipping.
- **Options:** choices like Size or Colour, plus **See Also** and **Alternative products** shown on the product page.
- **Images:** drag in a main image and more pictures. JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP work.
- **Descriptions:** the main description, two more with their own images, and a **Video URL**.
- **Technical:** the product's specification details.

Press **Save Changes**. **View on Site** opens the live product page.

**Copy content** copies the descriptions from another product, and replaces what is there now, so check it first.
